# Dashboard changelog: moving onto the metric registry

Date: 2026-10-15. Plan step 2 (`spec-chatbot-v2-plan.md`). The dashboard's figures that have a registry metric are now
computed BY the registry (`registry/`), the one definition shared with the chatbot. `ui/queries.py` only adapts the
registry's result to the shape each chart already expects; function names and return shapes are unchanged, so
`ui/app.py` and `ui/charts.py` are untouched. Parity tests: `tests/test_registry_parity.py`.

## What a user will notice

1. **Feeding page: the numbers change.** It used to cost `feed_inventory.used_kg`; it now shows the registry's
   "feed consumed cost" (daily-log `feed_kg` priced from `feed_inventory`), the same figure as the Finance page.
   August poultry feed cost goes **869.66 to 642.70 (-26.1%)**, which is the Finance page's number. The two sources
   disagree month by month (table below): on this data **17 of 20 month-and-domain pairs differ by more than 5%** (measured against the daily-log figure), and
   the inventory records pig feed used in October 2025, a month before the first pig log entry (2025-11-02). The
   sync's reconciliation check will list these in the data-quality report; at launch it is **report-only** (no banner,
   no caveat on answers) with a per-farm tolerance (default 5%), to be calibrated on the first month of real data
   before it becomes a warning.
2. **FCR chart: the values change.** FCR is now feed kg / weight gained kg per batch between the batch's first and
   latest weight sample (a pig batch's starting weight counts as its first sample). A batch with fewer than two
   samples is left off the chart (the chatbot says why).
3. **Mortality rate: the way it is worked out changes.** Today the number on the card does not move, because it only
   looks at the last 30 days and nothing died in them (it reads 0.0% before and after). The difference shows the
   first time animals do die, so here is what changes, in plain words.

   *The old way* divided the number of deaths by the animals on the farm on the last day of the period. That
   goes wrong whenever animals leave during the period. In **April 2026, 3 pigs died**. By 30 April a whole flock of
   500 chickens had been sold and was gone, and only 26 pigs were left, so the old card divided 3 by 26 and showed
   **11.54%**. That makes a bad month look far worse than it was, because the denominator ignores everyone who was on
   the farm earlier in the month.

   *The new way* divides deaths by **all the animals that were at risk during the period**: the animals already on the
   farm when it started plus any that arrived during it. For April that is 526 animals, so 3 deaths is **0.57%**,
   and the answer says which animals it counted ("526 at the start of the period + 0 added = 526"). Over all
   recorded time the farm has lost 51 of the 2,102 animals it has ever held, **2.43%**, where the old way would have
   said 9.79% (51 divided by the 521 still here).

   The card also now counts the **first day** of a selected date range, which it used to leave out.
4. **Domain Lookup page: feed totals are exact.** They were a sum of rounded monthly values and could differ from the
   Finance page by cents; they now equal it exactly.
5. **Finance page: unchanged.** Feed cost by domain is identical in both scenarios, with money rounding done half-up
   on exact decimals. (An early version of the registry rounded through binary floats and moved the all-time
   poultry figure by one cent, 4781.46 to 4781.45. The comparison caught it; a test now pins it.)

Everything else (breeding, debtors, expense breakdown, expenses vs revenue, findings, deaths by month, headcount by
month, health summary, crops, the stat cards today) is identical before and after; the list is at the end of the
generated section.

Not moved onto the registry: **Headcount by month** (a month-end trend chart, a different view from the headcount
snapshot) and **Findings** (checked against the registry by a parity test instead).

## Mortality: old way against the new way, on periods that have deaths

Old = deaths divided by the animals on the farm on the last day of the period. New = deaths divided by the animals
at risk (on the farm at the start, plus any added). The April 2026 example is explained in point 3 above.

| window | deaths | old: deaths ÷ headcount at window end | new: deaths ÷ animals at risk | at risk (opening + added) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 2025 | 1 | 0.18% (÷ 554) | 0.18% | 34 + 520 = 554 |
| Jan 2026 | 42 | 8.20% (÷ 512) | 7.58% | 554 + 0 = 554 |
| Apr 2026 | 3 | 11.54% (÷ 26) | 0.57% | 526 + 0 = 526 |
| May 2026 | 2 | 5.26% (÷ 38) | 5.26% | 26 + 12 = 38 |
| Jan to Mar 2026 | 42 | 7.98% (÷ 526) | 3.89% | 554 + 526 = 1,080 |
| All recorded time | 51 | 9.79% (÷ 521) | 2.43% | 0 + 2,102 = 2,102 |
