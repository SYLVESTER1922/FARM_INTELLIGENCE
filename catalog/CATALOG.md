## What the bot cannot answer

### Not recorded

- **accounts_payable** (money the farm OWES others): This farm doesn't track money it owes to suppliers or others (accounts payable). Only money that customers owe the farm is recorded, and I can show that. To answer this, log supplier invoices with the amount, the due date and whether each one is paid.
- **current_sickness** (whether animals are sick right now): Whether an animal is sick right now isn't recorded; only health events (treatments, vaccinations and inspections) are logged, and I can show those. To answer this, log a sick-animal status for each batch, with a date.
- **forecast_weather** (future weather): Only past weather is logged; there is no forecast. I can show the latest logged day, or rainfall over a period.
- **equipment** (machinery, tractors, vehicles): Equipment, machinery and vehicle records (such as service dates) aren't tracked.
- **company_info** (ownership, legal, registration): Company ownership, legal structure and registration details aren't recorded here.
- **payroll_history** (what each person was paid and when): Payroll history (what each person was paid, and when) isn't recorded; only the labour hours logged are, and I can show labour cost and hours. To answer this, log each payment with the person, the amount and the date.

### Recorded, but no metric reads it yet

- **staff_info** (who works on the farm and their roles): Staff names and roles are recorded, but I can't answer questions about them yet. I can show labour cost, hours worked and overtime.
- **crop_scouting** (crop pest, disease, weed or growth-stage scouting records): Crop scouting records (pests, diseases, growth stage, soil moisture) are logged, but I can't answer questions about them yet. I can show the land planted, the crops grown and harvest yields.
- **field_operations** (crop field work: spraying, irrigation, applications, machine hours): Field operations (sprayings, fertiliser and chemical applications, irrigation, machine and labour hours for each planting) are logged, but I can't answer questions about them yet. I can show what was spent on fertiliser and chemicals, and harvest yields.

### Not shown, by choice

- **staff_pay** (what an individual person is paid: pay rates, salary, wage per worker): I don't show what individual people are paid. I can show total labour cost, hours worked and overtime for the farm or for a section.

### Not allowed

- **write_action** (a request to change, add or delete data): I can only read your farm's data; I can't change, add or delete records.

### Anything else

- I can only answer questions about this farm's piggery, poultry and crops records, such as headcount, mortality, feed, expenses, revenue and who owes you money.

## Tables no metric reads

- `farm_profile` (internal): The farm's own settings: name, currency, which modules are on.
- `field_operations` (tracked_no_metric): Crop field work: applications, irrigation, machine and labour hours per planting.
- `list_values` (internal): The drop-down lists the workbook uses; not farm data.
- `scouting_log` (tracked_no_metric): Crop scouting: pests, diseases, growth stage, soil moisture.
- `staff` (tracked_no_metric): Staff names, roles and pay rates. Decision (plan 24.2): chat never shows per-person pay (the staff_pay refusal); names and roles are recorded but no metric reads them yet.
